Manual Entry (from a printed Nautical Almanac)

Enter the tabulated latitude band above and below your AP once -- it's reused for all four rise/set events, the way a real almanac page is laid out. Meridian passage doesn't depend on latitude, so just enter it directly. Times are Local Mean Time (LMT), as tabulated. Civil and Nautical Twilight are calculated automatically from Sunrise, Sunset, and Meridian Passage above -- no separate entry needed.

Signed decimal degrees (N positive, S negative)

Moon longitude correction (optional). Unlike the Sun, the Moon's tabulated rise/set/transit time drifts by up to ~1 hour a day, which matters once you're any real distance from Greenwich. To correct for it, also enter the Moon's tabulated time for the adjacent Greenwich date -- the following date if your AP is in West longitude, or the preceding date if East. Leave these blank to skip the correction (matches the old behavior).

Auto-fill from USNO

USNO reports Civil Twilight directly; Nautical Twilight isn't part of this data service, so it's calculated from the fetched Sunrise/Sunset/Meridian Passage instead.
